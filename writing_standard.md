# Writing standard (the coaching rubric)

PLACEHOLDER. The full SMART rubric and before/after examples land with the
coaching loop (T3). It will encode: a task or weekly priority must be Specific
(a concrete action verb), Measurable (a number or verifiable deliverable),
Achievable at the right altitude (a meaningful week of work, not a one-hour
subtask or an unaccomplishable epic), Relevant (the person can state how it moves
their metric), and Time-bound (a date or window).

Anchor examples from Kayvon:
- Pass: "Confirm renewal for 15 whitelisters in June and 25 for July."
- Fail: "A conversation from a campaign through the summer."

This file is loaded verbatim into the coaching system prompt, so it is the single
biggest lever on coaching quality. Keep it explicit and example-rich.
